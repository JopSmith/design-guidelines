# AILabel

## 1. Description

An AILabel is a clickable UI element used to clearly show when and how AI is being used. It has a consistent visual identity, paired with clear contextual explanations, which helps users understand what content is AI-generated and what is human-created, reinforcing confidence and clarity throughout the experience.

## 2. Usage

### When to Use

Use AILabels to provide transparency and reassurance in areas where AI plays a role in creating, modifying, or suggesting content. It should appear close to the relevant output so users can quickly understand its origin without interrupting their flow. AILabels are especially useful in trust-sensitive contexts, helping users make informed decisions and feel confident about how AI contributes to the experience.

### Do
- Use the AILabel to identify AI-generated content or actions
- Place the AILabel near the element it relates to (e.g., form label or dialog title)
- Pair the AILabel with a brief tooltip or explanation to clarify how the content was generated
- Ensure the AILabel remains visible and accessible in all themes and contrast modes

### Don't
- Hide AI usage or generate content without clear labelling
- Separate the label far from the AI-triggered component
- Overload the AILabel with long descriptions or unnecessary detail
- Use colour or styling that makes the AILabel hard to read or easy to overlook
