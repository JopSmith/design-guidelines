# Footer

## 1. Description

A Footer component is a persistent section positioned at the bottom of a page that provides supporting content and actions. It often includes secondary navigation, contextual links, legal information, or primary and secondary action buttons related to the current view. A Footer helps anchor important information and ensures consistent access to key controls without disrupting the main content area.

## 2. Usage

### When to Use

Use a Footer when actions or supplementary information need to remain consistently accessible as users engage with content. It is meant for lower-frequency, trust-building, or regulatory content rather than core task flows.

### Do
- Use the Footer for globally relevant content such as legal information or persistent navigation
- Keep the layout consistent across the entire product
- Ensure primary global actions are clearly prioritised
- Maintain clear visual separation from the main content area
- Design for responsiveness across screen sizes
- Keep content concise and purposeful
- Group related links into clear vertical lists or columns
- Include company branding or logo to indicate ownership and trust
- Keep link text concise and predictable, e.g. Privacy policy, Contact us, etc

### Don't
- Place highly contextual or page-specific content in the global Footer
- Vary structure or positioning between pages without clear reason
- Overcrowd the Footer with too many competing actions
- Allow the Footer to blend into page content and become indistinct
- Let content wrap awkwardly or become inaccessible on smaller screens
- Use the Footer as a catch-all area for miscellaneous links or information
- Present a long, unstructured list of mixed links with no clear grouping
- Use the Footer to introduce unrelated marketing campaigns or large promotional banners
- Use vague or playful wording for compliance items
