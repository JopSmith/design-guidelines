# Rating

## 1. Description

A Ratings component is a star-based rating system that enables users to evaluate content and express their opinion in a clear and quantifiable way. It can capture an individual user's score and may also display an aggregated score based on multiple user submissions. This helps communicate overall sentiment and gives others a quick indication of quality or relevance.

## 2. Usage

### When to Use

Use a Ratings component when you need to gather structured feedback or present a summary of community opinion. It is particularly useful for products, articles, services, or features where users benefit from seeing measurable sentiment at a glance and where lightweight interaction is more appropriate than detailed written feedback.

### Do
- Use a star-based rating system to capture high-level user sentiment
- Clearly indicate the scale such as 1 to 5 stars
- Keep the number of stars consistent across the product
- Provide a clear hover and selected state for each star
- Present aggregated star ratings clearly with total number of ratings
- Allow users to update their star rating where appropriate
- Use sparingly to avoid overwhelming users
- Ensure stars are large enough to tap comfortably on touch devices

### Don't
- Use star ratings as the sole method for critical decision making
- Leave the scale ambiguous or undefined
- Change the star scale between different screens or contexts
- Make it unclear which star is selected
- Display star averages without context or volume
- Prevent users from correcting accidental selections
- Prompt users for star ratings too frequently
- Make stars too small or difficult to select
