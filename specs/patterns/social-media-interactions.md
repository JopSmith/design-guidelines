# Social Media Interactions

## 1. Description

A Social media interactions pattern is a compact set of standardised actions presented alongside content or messages, typically as a row of icons. It enables users to quickly engage with content through familiar actions such as liking, commenting, saving, or copying, without interrupting their flow.

## 2. Usage

Use this pattern in feeds, messaging, or content-driven interfaces where users are expected to frequently interact with items in lightweight ways. It works best when actions are consistent, easily recognisable, and require minimal effort, allowing users to react, explore conversations, or manage content quickly and intuitively.

### Do

- Use familiar, widely recognised icons for common actions
- Keep actions concise and limited to the most important interactions
- Place interactions consistently in relation to content or messages
- Provide clear feedback for interactions (e.g. liked state)
- Ensure actions are easy to tap or click, especially on mobile
- Prioritise frequently used actions in the most prominent positions

### Don't

- Introduce unclear or non-standard icons
- Overload the pattern with too many actions
- Move or vary placement between items
- Leave users unsure if their action was successful
- Use small or tightly spaced touch targets
- Hide key actions behind additional interactions unnecessarily

## 3. Types

### 3.1 News Interactions

Actions associated with posts or content in a news feed.

| Type | Used For |
|------|----------|
| Like | Expressing a positive reaction to a post |
| Comments | Viewing the comment thread associated with a post |
| Save | Bookmarks a post for viewing later |

**Guidelines:**

- Place interactions close to the related news item
- Keep interactions in a consistent and predictable order
- Update counts or states immediately after interaction
- Use clear visual states for active and inactive interactions
- Use subtle animations for certain interactions (e.g. Like) to create small visual "delighters" that enhance feedback without distracting from the content

### 3.2 Chat Interactions

Actions associated with messages in conversations.

| Type | Used For |
|------|----------|
| Copy | Copying a message to the clipboard for reuse elsewhere |

**Guidelines:**

- Place chat interactions close to the relevant message
- Provide immediate feedback when a message is copied, e.g. message copied
